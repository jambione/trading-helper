# Plan from 2026-10-06: decision gates to "ahead at the end of the day"

**Where we are**
- **Day desk:** entries are as good as a random minute (everything tested). Gross is about 0 bp per trade, and net is
  about −5 to −8 bp (spread). After the 0.05% cap the half-spread is only about 1.5–2.6 bp per side.
- **Overnight book:** the one real edge. The ranking was confirmed on 2000–2015, but the profit beyond the market is
  unproven, so the live account stays at $100.
- **The road to profit** is cost and round trips, plus a name supply that is tight and still moving. A better
  indicator is not on that road.

**Rules for the whole plan**
1. **One change at a time.** Each change gets about 10 sessions before the next one lands. The settings freeze runs
   until 10/15.
2. **Every test is pre-registered before its data.** Any PASS goes to the skeptic before it is acted on. A FAIL is
   written down plainly and the idea is dropped.
3. **The day desk stays on paper** until gate G-LIVE. Live money starts small and grows only on real fills.

## G-LIVE: when the day desk may use real money
- **20 consecutive paper sessions** on one fixed configuration.
- Net after real spreads above 0 with day-clustered t ≥ 2.
- No single day worse than the daily loss brake (restore 3.0R first).
- Then live at the smallest size, scored with the tape-honest fill check, for 20 more sessions before any increase.
- Earliest possible: **mid-to-late November** if every gate goes well.

## Overnight book (the main book)
- **Keep $100 live** and log every real auction fill.
- **Gate O1 (~40 live nights, about late November):**
  - measured round-trip cost ≤ 4 bp (the backtest's assumption);
  - live returns track the paper book (correlation ≥ 0.8).
  - If both hold, discuss a larger account. The decision is the operator's, sized to what can be lost.
- **Not before O1:** no hedging, no new variants. The ranking is real; the money beyond market exposure is unproven.

- Paid data, until G2 shows passive orders work.

## Honest expectation
- If G1, G2 and G5 all pass, the day desk can reach about break-even to a few bp per trade. That is worth reaching, but
  at a $2,000 account it is a few dollars a day.
- Income-level results need a proven edge and more capital. Overnight (gate O1) is the more likely path.
- Nothing here should be relied on for household income until G-LIVE and O1 are both passed.
