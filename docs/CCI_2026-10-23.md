# CCI on 2026-10-23: four buys inside a gap fade

**Day.** CCI closed 78.83 the day before and opened **91.02 (+15.5%)**. The day high (91.56) printed in the first minute. From there it faded to 85.00 at 15:27 and closed at 85.99, still up 9.1%. It was on the desk's board from 09:32 to 15:49 and was checked about every 12 s (1,377 arm polls).

**Why the other 1,369 polls did not buy:**

| Reason | Polls |
|---|---|
| exhaustion falling | 558 |
| exhaustion not tight | 317 |
| waiting for exhaustion | 186 |
| spread too wide | 174 |
| tape only | 84 |
| stale square | 37 |
| %R not live | 13 |

**What bought.** Four times, the **pre-square** arm fired (`last_presquare`): fast %R rising, exhaustion "heating", RSI 57-81. Each was a market buy of 4 shares (~$350).

| Buy | Run-up into the buy (30 min) | Bought vs that 30-min high | Held | Best / worst while held | Result | If held 30 min |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10:30 @ 88.50 | +120 bp | 47 bp below | 2 min | +25 / −45 | **+2 bp** | −275 bp |
| 11:43 @ 88.15 | +172 bp | **3 bp below** | 12 min | +12 / −61 | **−66 bp** | −92 bp |
| 14:11 @ 86.79 | +70 bp | **0 bp (at the high)** | 15 min | +7 / −53 | **−56 bp** | −42 bp |
| 14:47 @ 86.81 | +10 bp | 5 bp below | 4 min | +24 / −20 | **+13 bp** | −132 bp |

**Total:** −$3.76 on four ~$350 trades, about −107 bp combined.

**Reading:**
- **Where in the run the desk entered.** Every buy came at the END of a 1-2% relief bounce inside a stock fading from a +15% gap, at or within 0.5% of the bounce's top. The arm needs %R to have turned up, so by the time it fires the bounce is mostly spent. The desk was buying the top of each bounce, not the start of a run.
- **What happened next.** Price kept falling: −42 to −275 bp in the next 30 minutes and −92 to −284 bp by the close.
- **What the exits did.** The trailing exits held each loss to 66 bp or less and banked two small gains. **The exits saved this trade; the entries were the problem**, the same split the studies show (exits keep the edge, entries have none).
- **The new sideways rule.** It has been live since 16:10 on 10/23 and requires fast %R up 15 points over 10 minutes. It would probably NOT have blocked these, because each came after a sharp bounce, not a sideways drift.
- **Related evidence.** A big gap-up that fades all day matches "the universe fades" and "dips after a signal are the fall".
