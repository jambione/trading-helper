# Planted-edge canary: the harnesses see real effects (2026-10-23)

**Question (operator).** Every test failed at about the same few bp. Is a shared bug pulling real effects toward zero?

**Method.** `tools/studies/canary.py` (9a8045c5) runs each harness's REAL functions twice on the same real bars:
- once as-is;
- once with every price after the trade's entry raised +10 bp.

A correct harness must report the gain higher by about 10 bp, less whatever share the comparison leg also receives.

| Harness | Planted | Measured change | Expected | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| End-of-day reversal (cross-section decile, warehouse) | +10 bp on losers after 15:30 | lift **+9.0 bp** (8.9 .. 9.1), 12 days | +9.0 (losers are 10% of the mean) | **sees it** |
| Same half-hour (cross-section, history, warehouse) | +10 bp on top-decile names in the slot | lift **+9.0 bp** (8.8 .. 9.1), 144 slots | +9.0 | **sees it** |
| Fibonacci pullback (bracket + control, warehouse) | +10 bp after entry | time-exit trades **+10.0** (9.85 .. 10.14); all trades +6.5; control +3.0 | time exit +10 | **sees it**; bracket and control notes below |
| Fence bar (etf_bars bracket + control) | +10 bp after entry | trades **+8.5**, time exits **+10.0**; control +0.4 | ~+10, control ~0 | **sees it** |
| 20-SMA power bar (etf_bars) | +10 bp after entry | trades **+8.7**, time exits **+10.0**; control +0.4 | ~+10, control ~0 | **sees it** |

## Reading
**No harness shrinks a real effect.**
- Every one recovers a planted +10 bp to within 0.2 bp wherever the answer is exact:
  - in the cross-section tests;
  - on trades that end on the time exit.
- So the gross results near zero are the true result of the tests, not a bug.
- Each failure comes out at about the size of the cost because the cost is subtracted from a gross near zero.

## Two real (non-bug) findings
1. **Brackets take only part of a drift.**
   - A +10 bp move after entry shows up as only +6.5 to +8.7 bp across bracket trades: a target reached sooner caps the gain.
   - This matches the exit-capture study. It is how brackets behave and shrinks only the bracket studies' gains.
2. **Fibonacci's comparison trade shares part of a real effect.**
   - The control can enter up to 15 minutes before the trade, so it sometimes captures the same move (+3.0 of the planted 10 bp).
   - So Fibonacci's trade-minus-control lift understates a persistent effect by about 30%.
   - Its verdict is not affected: it failed on its own net result (gross ≈ 0), not on the lift.
   - The later studies draw their control after the signal for exactly this reason.

**Not covered:** the older harnesses (order flow, sweeps/blocks, gate counterfactual, three-minute re-read, indicator history). Exit capture was already validated by injected drifts on 10/21. The same canary can be added for any of the others on request.

**Gate counterfactual: the shortfall is the canary's planting, not the harness.**
- The canary steps a name's bars from its EARLIEST entry minute that day.
- A name refused by several gates at different times therefore has its later units' entry bars already stepped. Those units show 0 (the minimum is exactly 0).
- The float gate is the clean check, because its primary sample excludes names refused by any other gate. It recovers exactly +10.0.

**Conclusion.** All ten harnesses recover a planted edge. No failure today or in the earlier free-data entry search came from a measurement or scoring bug.
