## What it says

- **The exits do not turn the turn into a profit.** Opening every turn more than doubles the trades (986 vs 445)
  at the same per-trade loss, about minus the spread. turn5 loses about 2.6x as much per day as live, and that gap
  is significant on both price lines.
- **turn20 looks "better than live" only because each position is a quarter of the size.** On its own it loses
  money with t -3. Smaller bets on a losing trade lose less; that is not an edge.
- **The winners are not allowed to run.** 90% of all exits in every arm are `local_trail` (the ratchet/leash);
  the exit stack sells fast, so a turn entry gets the spread cost and little of any run. This matches
  right_tail_runner (2026-10-15) and prescreen_turn (2026-10-17).
- **The live reference itself is negative** (-3.8 bp/trade, t -2.2, both halves) on these 9 sessions.
- The replay is a model: its live arm opened 13-69 a day where the recorded desk filled 21-98.
