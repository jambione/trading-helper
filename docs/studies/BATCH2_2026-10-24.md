## Reading
1. **The bottleneck is the arm rule's waiting.**
   - Execution is fast: 11 s, about 3 bp.
   - The IEX feed is not late: 0 s versus SIP.
   - The arm fires about 14 minutes and about 24 bp after %R has turned. By then about two thirds of the 30-minute move from the low is gone. That is the CCI picture.
2. **Moving the entry to the turn helps a little but not enough.**
   - Entering at the turn by rule (the trend trade) is a random minute.
   - Entering on a buy-stop through the oversold minute's high (T2) is the best entry family we have found: +2 to +2.4 bp vs random, t 5-10, in both halves and against both controls. But gross is only +1.9 / +2.8 bp, below a 3-5 bp round trip.
3. **Nothing in batch 2 passes.** T2 is "real but small", the same pattern as order flow (+1.5-2 bp). Two small real effects now exist: buy-stop timing and buyer-initiated flow. Whether they add up, and whether a cheaper exit or entry can carry them past cost, is the one open question with any evidence behind it.
