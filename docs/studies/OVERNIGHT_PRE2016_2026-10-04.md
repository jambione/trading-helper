# Overnight book on 2000–2015: the S2 ranking edge replicates (amended check); S1 passes the registered bar but is UNPROVEN as alpha

**Skeptic review (checkpoint 2, 2026-10-04): S2 CONFIRMED** as an overnight ranking fact. It is gross, not P&L.
**S1 UNPROVEN.** It meets the letter of the amended pre-registration, but the evidence a live decision needs is
missing:
- It comes from P1 only.
- It leans on a survivor-universe excess.
- It is about 0 with a hedge that can be sized in real time.

**Reading:** this result does **not** justify a larger live account. S2 shows the ranking carries overnight
information. Whether that information can be harvested (with a short leg, or net of the intraday giveback) is
untested.

- **S1 (alpha):** the book's intercept on SPY overnight, less the cost of the hedge leg.
- **S2 (ranking):** picks minus all eligible names, regressed on SPY overnight.

## Skeptic findings (reruns on the mini)
- **S1 decomposition.** S1 = S2 (+8.59) + the universe's alpha vs SPY (+0.99) − 4 bp cost − the hedge-leg cost
  (2.26 × 1.21).
- **Universe vs SPY overnight:** +0.99 bp overall (t 2.4). By half: **P1 +1.74**, P2 +0.28. In 2016–26 it lags SPY.
  The P1 excess is the signature of survivorship, which the 2018–26 calibration cannot see: its horizon is short, and
  its "full" side has no delisting returns. Without that excess, S1 ≈ +1.9 (t ≈ 1.8).
- **Against S1 as alpha:**
  - P2 is +0.55 (t 0.47), so a pass on "P2 > 0" is not replication.
  - 2018–26: +1.71 (t 0.6).
  - Trailing-beta hedge: +0.76 (t 0.83).
  - Dropping the top 5 names: t 2.19.
- **The lower nightly SD is real, not an artifact.**
  - The same Yahoo pipeline on 2016–26 gives a residual SD of 120.8 bp, matching Alpaca's ~122, versus 65.7 bp in
    2000–15.
  - The picks' median |ON| is 50–65 bp/night in 2001–06, versus 160–205 in 2020–26.
  - SPY's overnight SD in 2004–06 was 24–32 bp.
- **Stale opens do not drive S2.**
  - ON==0 is rarer among picks than in the universe (2003: 8.0% vs 13.1%).
  - S2 on nonzero-ON name-nights only: +9.2 (t 10.1), versus +8.96 with zeros included.
- **S2 concentration.**
  - Dropping the top 5 contributing names (BB, BIDU, AXON, LVS, RMBS): +8.02 (t 9.4).
  - Dropping the top 3 nights: +8.35.
  - The same pipeline on 2016–26: +9.7.

## Plain words
- **The momentum ranking has real overnight information.** On 16 years nobody had looked at, the top-20 names beat
  the other liquid names by about 8–9 bp a night after their market exposure, in 15 of 16 years. That matches
  2016–2026, and it is not caused by stale prices or a few names.
- **That is not money yet.**
  - S2 is a gross spread between the picks and the other liquid names.
  - The live book is long-only. Its edge over SPY is the ranking edge plus whatever the universe does against SPY,
    minus about 4 bp of auction cost and about 2.7 bp of hedge.
  - In these years the universe *beat* SPY overnight, mostly in 2000–07 and probably flattered by survivorship. What
    eats the ranking edge is **cost**, not the universe.
  - Net of that, alpha is about 0 in 2008–15, in 2018–26, and with a hedge you could size in real time.
- **The open question** is whether the ranking edge can be captured more cheaply. Two candidates, both untested:
  - a short leg in the universe itself (shorting costs money);
  - pairing it with the next day's intraday leg.

## Caveats
- Survivorship: delistings before 2016 are missing. The calibration says the effect is small, but it is calibrated on
  2018–2026.
- Yahoo opening prices, no auction-fill model, and costs of 4 bp (understated before ~2005).
- The check was amended after it failed. The amendment was skeptic-approved and made before any pre-2016 outcome.
- Nightly SD in these years is about half of 2016–2026's (explained above: lower overnight volatility then).
