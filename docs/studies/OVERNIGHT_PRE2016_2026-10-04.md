Pre-registration: [`overnight_pre2016_prereg.json`](overnight_pre2016_prereg.json). Written 4f2a626, amended before data
fa3bf43 and e52ac3f, and amended **after the data-source check failed** ffed74b (skeptic-approved, before any pre-2016

`overnight_pre2016_gap_decomp.py`. Data: Yahoo daily bars, git-ignored, on the mini only, in `data/yahoo_pre2016/`.

The book is frozen exactly as live: top-20 by 12-1 momentum, price ≥ $5, ADV20 ≥ $50M, close→next open, 4 bp cost.
- **S1 (alpha):** the book's intercept on SPY overnight, less the cost of the hedge leg.
- **S2 (ranking):** picks minus all eligible names, regressed on SPY overnight.
- SE = max(plain, Newey-West at 5 lags, Newey-West at 9 lags).

## Plain words (provisional)
- **The momentum ranking has real overnight information.** On 16 years nobody had looked at, the top-20 names beat the
  other liquid names by about 8–9 bp a night after their market exposure, nearly every year. That matches 2018–2026
  (+8.5 bp, t 3.3).
- **Hedged against SPY, the money is thinner.** After SPY beta and costs, alpha is +2.9 bp/night. But almost all of it
  is in 2000–2007, and it is about zero in 2008–2015 and with a hedge that is knowable in real time. The liquid
  universe as a whole lags SPY overnight, which eats most of the ranking edge.
- **The open question** is whether the ranking edge can be harvested more cheaply than with a SPY hedge. One option is
  a short leg in the universe itself, but shorting costs money and is untested.

## Caveats
- Survivorship: delistings before 2016 are missing. The calibration says the effect is small, but it is calibrated on
  2018–2026.
- Yahoo opening prices, no auction-fill model, and costs of 4 bp (understated before ~2005).
- The check was amended after it failed. The amendment was skeptic-approved and made before any pre-2016 outcome.
- **Nightly SD on these years is about half of 2016–2026's** (S1 SE 1.0 vs 2.8 bp). That is unexplained pending review.
